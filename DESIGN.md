# Design direction

Use standard shadcn-style controls and neutral Tailwind tokens. Geist is self-hosted through
its package. Light/dark follow the system. This is a working studio first: clear navigation,
legible prose, accessible controls, and a comfortable rich Markdown editor.

Keep desktop conversations spacious and mobile navigation usable. Every action needs a visible
state or result. Preserve keyboard focus, semantic labels and reduced-motion behavior. The
public holding page may use one quiet construction animation. Its identity is intentionally
minimal while the future public experience is designed.

Historical portfolio rules and copy are retired. Do not recreate them as implicit constraints.
