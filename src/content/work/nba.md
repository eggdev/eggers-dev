On NBA.com and League Pass, I built ScoreStrip from the first commit through
majority ownership: the live scores most visitors came to check, and the
site's most-used feature. I was the client-side tech lead for third-party login
and entitlements across web and connected devices, spanning TV Everywhere,
international broadcasters, and NBA's own CIAM identity, and packaged it into
@nbad/auth, a reusable React auth library I own most of.

I tech-led the League Pass purchase page and the recursive block-rendering system
behind it, so marketing could edit subscription and Local Access pages as content
blocks without an engineer, localized across three languages. I helped launch
Local Access in-market streaming that routed around blackouts, and built the Nike
sign-up flow for the 2024 All-Star booth in Indianapolis: around 25,000 sign-ups
over the weekend with zero production incidents.

Then I requested a rotation onto the platform team and built RepositoryManagementTool,
the self-service repo-provisioning tool, with security scanning and approvals
built in, that NBA's DTC org standardized on.
