I have spent a couple of years now getting machines to write software for me, and almost none of it in public. That stops here.

This is a build log. Not a news desk, not a tutorial mill — a record of what I am actually building, what broke, and what I learned, written honestly enough to be useful to the next person staring at the same error.

Lately the work has pulled local: running models on my own hardware instead of renting someone else's, mostly because I got tired of my stack being something another company could change or take away overnight. More on that soon.

One note on dates. Posts here are often retrospective — "here is a thing I figured out earlier this year." The date on a post is the day I published it. The story inside it is when it happened. I am not interested in faking a history; the real one is more interesting anyway.
