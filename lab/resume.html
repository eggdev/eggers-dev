Resume document lab

This lab chooses how rule 7 is drawn: the resume is the screen, notes sit beside the bullet they concern, every change the tool proposes can be rejected, and every claim links to its fact. Move a control, try Keep, Reject and Undo, open a fact from its number, then Save and send me the JSON. Content switches between few, typical and many notes. Under 640px wide a margin note always moves under its bullet.
