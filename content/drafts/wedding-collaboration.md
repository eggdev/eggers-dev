Every deadline I've shipped against professionally had some give in it, even when nobody admitted it. A wedding date doesn't. Ours is fixed, the guests are real, and the software we're planning it with is software I built.

The whole wedding lives in it. Emina and I plan the song list, the seating chart, the room block selections, and the honeymoon, and we manage the RSVPs and guest names from the same place. We removed Zola and The Knot from our workflows and made a custom project out of our big day, built with AI.

Emina is a product manager, and our planning looks like it: scope, tickets, priorities, and that date sitting at the end of every conversation. She runs the plan. I'm the engineering side of the two-person team.

It's the smallest team I've ever been on, and the retro happens at dinner. We find out on the day how well we built.
