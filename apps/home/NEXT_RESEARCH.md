# Next honeymoon research

The live research queue belongs in private D1. This file documents the reusable workflow only and
contains no traveler details, balances, dates, destinations, or award targets.

## Close the most urgent decision first

1. Read the current deadline and affected transfer path from the private points wallet.
2. Choose two or three valid trip windows from the private traveler profile.
3. Check the leading hotel candidates for live standard-award inventory, exact award room, current
   points price, and the cash price for that same room.
4. Add mandatory transfers, meal costs, and material fees.
5. Transfer only the points required by a useful, verified reservation.

## Build exact trip concepts

For each leading destination, keep relaxing, balanced, and active versions when geographically
sensible. Add at least one premium-cabin flight strategy and one credible hotel redemption before
promoting a destination to finalist.

## Required structured facts

For each serious concept, capture:

- flight program, operating carrier, cabin, points for two, taxes, route, and availability timestamp;
- hotel, actual award room, dates, points total, same-room cash rate, and breakfast terms;
- unavoidable cash by air fees, lodging, food, activities, and local/resort transfers;
- optional splurges separately;
- source URL, verification date, and confidence for every time-sensitive value.

Use the in-app research log as live checks are completed. Estimated or general facts should never be
upgraded to “verified current” without a date-specific check.
